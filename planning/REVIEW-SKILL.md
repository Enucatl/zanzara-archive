# Repository phase-review skill specification

The maintained implementation is [review-phase](../.agents/skills/review-phase/SKILL.md). Read that file when invoking or changing the skill; this document does not duplicate its instructions.

Reviews assess integrated behavior when requested or when material integration risk warrants one. Phase parents track milestones. They do not require a separate review invocation, exact-commit PASS, or user release phrase before ordinary authorized work can proceed. The current scope and workflow policy in [README](README.md) take precedence over historical release-gate language.

Review evidence is reusable while the relevant behavior, model/configuration, and inputs remain unchanged. Focused checks, real quality measurements, human decisions, and private evidence paths support conclusions. Routine archive/checkpoint/evidence rehashes and whole-Project reconciliation are unnecessary.

For skill edits, run the skill-creator frontmatter validator and inspect whether the instructions preserve scope, human decisions, privacy, and actual authorization requirements. Do not recreate synthetic release-ceremony fixtures or model-switch tests.
