## Outcome and requirement

Canonical storage and synthetic stage runners; output one durable worker and job CLI with claims, fencing and recovery.

Use [D3](../SYSTEM-DESIGN.md#d3--canonical-data-and-publication), [D4](../SYSTEM-DESIGN.md#d4--worker-and-invalidation), [D8](../SYSTEM-DESIGN.md#d8--shared-inference-provider-changes-and-money) and the existing contracts/modules produced by completed prerequisites. ## Prerequisites

Parent: [P0](P0.md). Required completed blockers: [P0-04](P0-04.md). This issue does not depend on its own parent closing.

## Bounded steps

1. Implement stage keys from source/upstream/model/config/pipeline hashes and the explicit D4 dependency graph.
2. Implement short transactional claims, 120-second leases, 30-second heartbeats, fencing tokens, bounded retries and failure classes.
3. Expose enqueue/status/retry/cancel commands, recover expired leases and reconcile artifacts; paid ambiguous attempts remain reserved.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Canonical storage and synthetic stage runners; output one durable worker and job CLI with claims, fencing and recovery. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Duplicate claims/publication, invalidation leakage or unbounded retries block completion.

## Exclusions

No Redis, Celery, concurrent worker pool or real paid calls.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] A killed worker restarts from missing stages without duplicating completed artifacts.
- [ ] A stale lease holder cannot publish or mark a reclaimed job complete.
- [ ] Changing ASR leaves voice vectors reusable; changing diarization invalidates exemplars and attribution.
- [ ] Retry exhaustion, deterministic failures and cancellation have documented terminal states.
- [ ] Required evidence below is linked and applies to the reviewed commit/configuration.
