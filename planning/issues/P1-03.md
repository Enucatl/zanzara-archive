1. Run one episode-wide clustering pass through Community-1 behind Diarizer.
2. Persist standard turns, exclusive turns and derived overlap intervals with original millisecond offsets and model fingerprints.
3. Validate speaker IDs are scoped to the episode/run; expose private RTTM-compatible evaluation exports at the seconds boundary.
