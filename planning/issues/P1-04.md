## Bounded steps

1. Assign words by greatest exclusive-turn intersection, midpoint then stable-ID ties; preserve unassigned words.
2. Annotate overlap independently using standard turns and retain exact word IDs/time offsets.
3. Generate structured transcript API payload and derived TXT/SRT/VTT exports with speaker labels.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Timed ASR words plus both diarization representations; output attributed JSON and TXT/SRT/VTT renderers. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Unusable word times or inconsistent diarization artifact provenance blocks attribution.

## Exclusions

No identity inference, text indexing or claimed reference-quality transcript.
