# [P1R-03B] Use exclusive diarization turn boundaries to reduce hard-max chunking

- Stable ID: P1R-03B
- Executor: Luna
- Kind: Implementation
- Parent: P1R
- Blocked by: P0, P1R-02, P1R-03A, P1-03

Superseded by P1R-03D. Retain this issue and its implementation history for provenance; do not complete the obsolete algorithm or validation path.

## Outcome and requirement

Revise the P1R adaptive chunker after the v4 distribution produced 4,304 chunks with mean 22.47 s, median 25.54 s, and 1,819 (42.3%) 30-second hard-max chunks. The intended behavior is practical review units centered near 12 seconds, retaining longer chunks only when speech structure provides no useful boundary.

This variant uses Community-1 exclusive diarization for speaker-turn boundary candidates and standard diarization for speech activity, zero-speaker gaps, true overlap measurement, overlap penalties, and diagnostics. A speaker transition remains usable even when standard diarization shows simultaneous speech nearby.

## Fixed configuration

    preferred_min_ms = 8_000
    target_ms = 12_000
    preferred_max_ms = 18_000
    soft_max_ms = 20_000
    hard_max_ms = 30_000
    strong_gap_ms = 600
    short_gap_ms = 250
    overlap_margin_ms = 250
    minimum_chunk_ms = 4_000

All values are versioned and included in the segmentation configuration fingerprint.

## Candidate generation

Generate exactly these natural candidate types:

- Type A: strong zero-speaker gap (>=600 ms) with different exclusive speakers before/after.
- Type B: strong zero-speaker gap (>=600 ms) without an exclusive speaker change.
- Type C: short zero-speaker gap (250 ms <= duration <600 ms) with different exclusive speakers.
- Type D: any exclusive A -> B transition with A != B, without requiring a gap.
- Type E: short zero-speaker gap (250 ms <= duration <600 ms) without a speaker change.
- Type F: artificial hard fallback only.

Ignore gaps below 250 ms as standalone candidates. Gap timestamps are deterministic integer-millisecond midpoints. Exclusive candidates use the exact transition timestamp in the artifact.

## Scoring and selection

Base costs are exactly: Type A 0.0, Type B 1.0, Type C 1.5, Type D 2.0, and Type E 3.0. In the [S+8,000, S+18,000] preferred window add distance_cost = abs(candidate_ms - (S+12,000))/1,000.0. Inspect standard overlap in [candidate_ms-250, candidate_ms+250] and add exactly 0.0 when there is none, 1.0 when overlap is nearby but not at the instant, or 2.0 when the candidate instant is inside >=2-speaker overlap. Overlap is a penalty, never a rejection rule.

Select by total score, lower base cost, lower overlap penalty, smaller target distance, then earlier timestamp. In (S+18,000, S+20,000], choose the earliest candidate in the highest available priority class: A, B, C, D, E. At 20 seconds, if an exclusive transition occurred in the 18–20 second interval, use the best transition by lowest overlap penalty then earliest timestamp, even if overlap-conflicted. This prevents conversational audio from drifting toward 30 seconds.

Extension beyond 20 seconds is allowed only when there has been no usable strong gap, short gap, or exclusive transition since S. Between 20 and 30 seconds select the first natural candidate encountered. If none exists, cut exactly at S+30,000 with boundary_end_reason hard_maximum; no chunk may exceed 30 seconds.

Retain P1R-03A final-remainder behavior: a remainder shorter than 8 seconds is merged into the previous chunk when the merged chunk remains <=30 seconds, otherwise it becomes a short terminal chunk.

## Diagnostics and outputs

Persist candidate_source, base_cost, distance_cost, overlap_penalty, and total_score where applicable. Persist exclusive_transition_count, standard_overlap_ms, and hard_max_reason when relevant. Every hard-max boundary also persists exclusive_transition_count_8_20s, gap_candidate_count_8_20s, overlap_conflicted_transition_count_8_20s, and the most specific reason from continuous_same_speaker, no_standard_gap, no_exclusive_transition, only_invalid_candidates, or unknown.

Persist manifest metadata counts for chunks ending with each Type A–F boundary and include them in the before/after distribution report.

## Acceptance

Run the v4 corpus and report total, mean, median, p90, p95, 8–18 s fraction, 18–20 s percentage, 20–30 s percentage, exactly-30 s percentage, hard-max reasons, and Type A–F counts. The gates are median 10–16 s, mean <18 s, >=70% in 8–18 s, and <10% exactly 30 s; target hard-max fraction is <5% and mandatory fraction is <10%.

Inspect at least 100 real-audio boundaries, including at least 20 clean exclusive changes, 20 exclusive changes with nearby overlap, 20 gap boundaries, 20 AST-music chunks, and 20 chunks longer than 18 seconds. Classify good, acceptable, awkward, or bad. Compare at least 30 former P1R-03A hard-max examples that become exclusive-transition boundaries. Do not claim completion when required real-audio or human evidence is unavailable.

Required synthetic cases cover: overlap-conflicted exclusive transition at 12.2 s; clean transition at 11.8 s scoring 2.2; strong-gap tie at 13.0 s beating exclusive at 12.0 s by lower base cost; target proximity beating a strong gap at 17.5 s; transitions at 18.7 and 19.4 s after the preferred window; genuine monologue hard-max at 30 s with continuous_same_speaker; first natural boundary at 23.1 s; transitions at 10.5/14.0/19.0 preventing hard max; and byte-identical deterministic reruns.

If exclusive diarization is missing or inconsistent with episode bounds, block generation for that episode and never silently fall back to P1R-03A. If standard diarization is missing, block generation because gaps and overlap cannot be trusted. Do not add another VAD, change Community-1 or AST, run ASR, create transcript gold, or perform semantic sentence segmentation.
