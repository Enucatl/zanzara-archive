# [P1R-03D] Native Community-1 speech-activity adaptive chunking

- Stable ID: P1R-03D
- Executor: Luna
- Kind: Implementation
- Parent: P1R
- Blocked by: P0, P1R-02, P1-03

## Outcome and requirement

Replace the P1R-03A and P1R-03B chunk-boundary algorithms with a simpler adaptive chunker based on the native speech-activity signal already computed inside Community-1. P1R-03D supersedes both previous attempts and must produce human-reviewable chunks centered around approximately 12 seconds.

P1R-03D is a prerequisite for the P1 release/review because it redoes the Community-1-dependent chunking portion of that release. It does not depend on completion of the superseded P1R-03A or P1R-03B implementations.

Natural structure may move a boundary away from exactly 12 seconds, but must not make the chunker wait many additional seconds for an ideal silence or speaker transition. Duration target is primary; native speech pauses, speaker transitions, and overlap information improve placement but do not control whether a chunk can end. The exact 12-second target is an ideal scoring reference, not a cut candidate.

The v4 baseline was 4,304 chunks, mean 22.47 s, median 25.54 s, and 1,819 30-second hard-max chunks (42.3%). P1R-03D must not repeat that failure mode.

## Design and interfaces

Extend the existing Community-1 diarization artifact and P1R chunk manifest contracts without removing standard or exclusive diarization provenance. P1-03 is the existing Community-1 service/runtime blocker named by the manifest; its native hook must be used during the existing episode-wide invocation.

## Bounded steps

1. Capture and persist native `speaker_counting` activity and reproducible frame-to-source timing metadata.
2. Implement versioned native-activity adaptive chunk selection and the P1R-03D manifest fields.
3. Add deterministic synthetic tests, native-activity validation, corpus distribution evidence, and boundary inspection evidence.

## Native Community-1 activity

Capture Community-1's native intermediate `speaker_counting` during the normal episode-wide diarization pipeline invocation using the supported pipeline hook mechanism. Do not infer activity solely from final `standard_turns`, run the segmentation network a second time, or access unstable private model internals.

Interpret `speaker_count == 0` as no detected speech, `== 1` as one active speaker, and `>= 2` as detected overlapping speech. This is an instantaneous derived speaker count, not a speech-probability score; do not expose or name it `speech_probability`.

One Community-1 inference must produce standard diarization, exclusive diarization, and native speaker-count activity. Persist native activity as deterministic source-relative run-length intervals, merging adjacent identical counts, for example:

    {"start_ms": 10520, "end_ms": 10840, "speaker_count": 0}
    {"start_ms": 10840, "end_ms": 12620, "speaker_count": 1}
    {"start_ms": 12620, "end_ms": 12910, "speaker_count": 2}

Persist frame/timeline metadata proving how native positions map to original episode milliseconds.

Use native activity for speech/no-speech transitions, pause duration, and native overlap; exclusive diarization for speaker ownership transitions; and standard diarization for final speaker structure, final overlap annotation, benchmark metadata, and cross-checking. Do not add Silero, MarbleNet, another pyannote VAD pipeline, or any other VAD model. AST remains responsible for music and acoustic conditions, never VAD.

## Fixed algorithm configuration

    preferred_min_ms = 8_000
    target_ms        = 12_000
    preferred_max_ms = 16_000
    hard_max_ms      = 30_000

    strong_pause_ms  = 400
    short_pause_ms   = 150
    overlap_margin_ms = 250
    minimum_chunk_ms = 4_000

P1R-03D retains 30 seconds as the absolute maximum. At 18 seconds the selector begins accepting progressively less-optimal structural candidates, with the relaxation increasing deterministically until the 30-second hard cut. All values and the relaxation policy are versioned and included in the segmentation configuration hash. No silent tuning on held-out data.

Relaxation is deterministic: from 18,000 through 24,000 ms select the earliest natural candidate whose overlap adjustment is <=0.75 (`relaxed_clean`); from 24,000 through 30,000 ms select the earliest natural candidate regardless of overlap (`relaxed_any`); if none exists by 30,000 ms cut exactly at 30,000 ms (`hard_maximum`). No target-distance scoring or candidate-type preference applies in either relaxation phase.

## Candidate generation

For a chunk beginning at S, ideal boundary T = S + 12,000 ms. Search structural candidates from S + 8,000 through S + 16,000 ms. T is used only as the distance reference; it is never emitted as a standalone target candidate.

Create:

- strong native pause: midpoint of every native speaker_count == 0 interval >=400 ms in range;
- short native pause: midpoint of every zero-count interval 150–399 ms in range;
- exclusive speaker transition: exact timestamp of every exclusive A -> B transition with A != B in range;
- combined pause plus speaker transition: mark a pause candidate `pause_and_speaker_change = true` when a transition is within +/-250 ms; do not create unnecessary duplicates.

Gaps below 150 ms are micro-gaps and do not become standalone candidates. Ordinary structural candidates before S + 8,000 ms are not selectable.

## Candidate scoring

Lower score wins:

    score = distance_cost + type_adjustment + overlap_adjustment
    distance_cost = abs(candidate_ms - target_ms) / 1000.0

Use exactly these single most-specific type adjustments:

    strong pause + speaker change   -2.50
    strong pause                    -2.00
    short pause + speaker change    -1.75
    speaker change                  -1.50
    short pause                     -1.00

Inspect native speaker_counting in candidate +/-250 ms:

    no count >=2 in interval                       +0.00
    count >=2 nearby but candidate instant <2     +0.75
    candidate instant count >=2                   +1.50

Overlap is a penalty and never makes a candidate invalid. A strong pause at 15.5 s scores 3.5 - 2.0 = 1.5; it remains selectable because no standalone target candidate exists. A speaker change at 11.4 s scores 0.6 - 1.5 = -0.9 and wins among the available structural candidates.

Tie-break equal scores by lower absolute target distance, stronger structural type adjustment, lower overlap adjustment, and earlier timestamp. No random tie-breaking.

## Episode end, ordinary minimum, and hard maximum

If episode end occurs before the selected boundary, use episode end. If the final remainder is shorter than 4,000 ms, merge it into the preceding chunk when the merged chunk remains <=30,000 ms; otherwise emit the short terminal chunk.

The chunk must end by S + 30,000 ms. At S + 18,000 ms the selector begins accepting less-optimal structural candidates, with increasing relaxation through the 30-second hard cut. No ordinary structural candidate before 8 seconds may be selected. When multiple candidate events share a timestamp, metadata selection priority is combined pause/speaker change, strong pause, short pause/speaker change, speaker change, then short pause; this priority never skips an earlier timestamp during relaxation.

## Required persisted fields

For every chunk persist chunk_id, episode_id, start_ms, end_ms, duration_ms, boundary_end_reason, selection_phase (`preferred`, `relaxed_clean`, `relaxed_any`, or `hard_maximum`), selected_candidate_type, selected_candidate_timestamp_ms, distance_cost, type_adjustment, overlap_adjustment, total_score, segmentation_version, segmentation_configuration_hash, community1_artifact_id, and native_activity_artifact_id.

For pause boundaries additionally persist pause_duration_ms. For speaker-transition boundaries persist exclusive_speaker_before and exclusive_speaker_after. Allowed principal reasons are strong_pause, short_pause, speaker_change, strong_pause_and_speaker_change, short_pause_and_speaker_change, episode_end, and hard_maximum.

## Native activity validation and music behavior

Before benchmark freeze, manually validate at least 200 short archive regions stratified approximately across speech only, speech plus background music, music/jingle without speech, overlap, overlap plus music, and difficult/noisy speech. Human truth is speech present/absent with optional overlap correctness. Report false speech on music-only, missed speech under music, missed ordinary speech, native zero-speaker precision, and native zero-speaker recall, split by AST no-music/background-music/dominant-music where available.

If music causes poor native decisions, preserve the measured result, keep structural speaker-change chunking functional, and disable/reduce native pause bonuses only through a separately versioned configuration. Do not reinterpret AST as VAD or add a VAD model without a separate planning decision. Do not fail chunk generation solely because pause detection is weak.

## Determinism and required synthetic tests

Identical source identity, Community-1 artifacts, native activity, and configuration must produce byte-identical manifests and matching SHA-256 values.

The following twelve cases are mandatory:

1. Continuous same-speaker speech: no target candidate is synthesized; use the documented relaxation/hard-maximum behavior.
2. Exclusive transition at 11.4 s without overlap: transition wins.
3. Exclusive transition at 15.5 s: select the 15.5 s structural candidate; target distance remains the scoring reference only.
4. Native zero-count 11.6–12.0 s (400 ms): midpoint 11.8 s strong pause wins.
5. Native zero-count 12.1–12.3 s (200 ms): valid short pause.
6. Native zero-count 80 ms: ignored standalone.
7. Pause midpoint near 12 s with changed exclusive speakers: combined adjustment.
8. Exact target in overlap versus clean transition at 11.5 s: transition wins when score is lower.
9. Target time in mild overlap versus only natural pause at 16 s: no target candidate is fabricated; select the available structural candidate.
10. AST dominant music with native speech active: no fabricated pause or music-specific segmentation.
11. Final short remainder: deterministic merge or terminal behavior.
12. Identical inputs: identical manifest SHA-256.

The relaxation-specific tests are also mandatory: a clean candidate at 18.2 s wins; an overlap-conflicted 18.2 s candidate yields to a clean 19.0 s candidate; a clean 23.0 s candidate wins over an overlap-conflicted 21.0 s candidate; an overlap-conflicted 24.4 s candidate is selected when no reasonable candidate exists before 24 s; a natural candidate at 27 s is selected; and no natural candidate through 30 s produces an exact 30 s hard cut. Each persists the corresponding selection_phase.

## Corpus acceptance and inspection

Run the same corpus used for v4. Required duration gates are mean 10–14 s, median 10–14 s, at least 90% of chunks in 8–16 s, fewer than 5% over 16 s, and fewer than 1% ending at hard maximum. Report 8–10, 10–12, 12–14, 14–16, 16–18, 18–20, 20–25, and 25–30 s bins, plus pause-selected, speaker-transition, combined, episode-end, and hard-max counts. There is no required pause-boundary percentage.

Inspect at least 150 real boundaries, including at least 30 exact-target, 30 speaker-change, 30 native-pause, 30 music-containing, and 30 overlap-containing chunks (categories may overlap). Classify good, acceptable, awkward, or bad. Compare examples from P1R-03A/P1R-03B that previously became very long; the expected improvement is shorter review units without materially worse cut quality.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Inputs are canonical episode audio identity, existing Community-1 pipeline, native speaker-count hook artifact, standard and exclusive diarization, AST condition labels where available, and the P1R chunk schema. Outputs are the extended Community-1 artifact with native activity, the P1R-03D chunk manifest, native-VAD archive validation report, duration comparison, and human boundary-quality report.

If the supported Community-1 hook cannot expose the artifact, stop and report the API/runtime blocker. Do not use unstable private internals. If frame-to-source mapping cannot be safely reproduced, stop. If Community-1 fails for an episode, block that episode rather than using ASR-derived boundaries. Do not add a VAD model, use ASR timestamps/punctuation, perform semantic segmentation, modify AST, evaluate transcription quality, create transcript gold, or require silence at every boundary.

## Exclusions

This task does not add another VAD model, use ASR timestamps or punctuation, perform semantic sentence segmentation, modify AST, evaluate transcription quality, create human transcript gold, or require every chunk to end on silence.
