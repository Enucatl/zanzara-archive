# [P1R-08] Derive speaker-count and overlap metadata from Community-1

- Stable ID: P1R-08
- Executor: Luna
- Kind: Implementation
- Parent: P1R
- Blocked by: P0, P1R-03

## Outcome and requirement

Attach speaker-structure metadata to every chunk from episode-wide `pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1` output for sampling, overlap-aware scoring, review prioritization and later speaker-attributed evaluation.

## Design and interfaces

Intersect each frozen chunk with the episode-wide standard and exclusive diarization artifacts; never diarize individual 10–15 second chunks. Standard tracks define real simultaneous speech. Persist `speaker_ids`, `speaker_count`, `max_simultaneous_speakers`, `speech_ms`, `overlap_ms`, `overlap_fraction`, `has_overlap`, and a versioned machine-seeded `speaker_condition`. `overlap_fraction` is undefined/not-applicable when `speech_ms` is zero. P1R-08A exclusively owns music and other acoustic-event evidence.

## Bounded steps

1. Intersect standard/exclusive turns with chunk intervals deterministically.
2. Calculate speech and overlap only from simultaneous standard tracks.
3. Freeze/version `single_speaker`, `multi_speaker_no_overlap`, `partial_overlap`, `heavy_overlap`, and `uncertain` thresholds before manifest freeze; allow reviewed speaker-count/overlap corrections separately.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Inputs are chunks and episode-wide Community-1 artifacts. Output is seeded speaker metadata. Missing diarization marks unknown rather than inventing labels; invalid interval geometry fails.

## Exclusions

No AST/music/audio-quality classification, unquestioned machine truth, speaker identity inference, or lexical scoring.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Community-1 runs episode-wide and frozen chunks are deterministically intersected with standard tracks.
- [ ] Fixtures cover zero, one, two and three simultaneous speakers, including chunk-edge entries/exits.
- [ ] Speaker count, maximum simultaneous speakers, speech/overlap milliseconds and fraction are reproducible.
- [ ] Reviewed speaker-count/overlap truth remains separate from machine seed.
- [ ] No music or generic audio-quality logic exists in this task.

## Verification commands

Run condition-metadata fixtures and focused persistence tests.

## Evidence and documentation

Record diarization fingerprint, threshold version, fixture geometry and metadata schema/version.

## Stop conditions and completion rule

Stop if standard overlap cannot be represented faithfully or episode-wide clustering is lost. Complete after derived and corrected speaker-metadata tests pass.
