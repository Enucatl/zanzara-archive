# [P1R-11] Freeze representative and stress benchmark manifests

- Stable ID: P1R-11
- Executor: Luna
- Kind: Evaluation
- Parent: P1R
- Blocked by: P0, P1R-10

## Outcome and requirement

Freeze a multi-episode benchmark of about 500 chunks: about 300 representative and 200 stress, with episode-level development/held-out independence and human approval of final membership.

## Design and interfaces

Representative selection reflects archive conditions without model-score filtering. Stress sampling deliberately includes overlap, music, overlap plus music, rapid turns, degraded audio, names/numbers and difficult transitions. Record source, chunk, selection reason, condition seed, partition and hashes.

## Bounded steps

1. Generate candidates from deterministic chunks/conditions.
2. Sample partitions by episode before model-selection/prompt-tuning decisions.
3. Present final private manifest for human approval and freeze it.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Input is eligible episode/chunk metadata. Output is immutable representative/stress and dev/held-out manifests. Insufficient condition coverage is reported; it is not hidden by relabeling.

## Exclusions

No ASR score-driven selection, adjacent same-conversation split, gold creation, or claimed statistical threshold.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Approximate composition and condition reasons are recorded with denominators.
- [ ] Dev/held-out grouping is episode-level and frozen before tuning.
- [ ] Representative and stress purposes are distinct.
- [ ] Human approval and manifest/source hashes are recorded.

## Verification commands

Run manifest validation, partition-leakage checks and deterministic sampling fixtures.

## Evidence and documentation

Record private manifest paths/hashes, condition counts/durations and approval record.

## Stop conditions and completion rule

Stop if episode grouping or model-independent selection cannot be proven. Complete after frozen approval and validation.
