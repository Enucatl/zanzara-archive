# [P2-01] Extract clean speaker exemplars

- Stable ID: P2-01
- Executor: Luna
- Kind: Implementation
- Parent: P2
- Blocked by: P1-03

Active under the 2026-09-28 working plan; direct prerequisites below replace phase-wide release gates.

## Outcome and requirement

Extract deterministic clean excerpts from episode-wide diarization, independently of transcription.

## Design and interfaces

Subtract overlaps and 250 ms at transitions, prefer 8–15 seconds, allow at least 3 seconds, retain at most ten excerpts per speaker. Preserve source offsets and exclusion reasons; speakers with no usable excerpt remain visible.
