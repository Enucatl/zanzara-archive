# [P2-09] Human identity links, contradiction checks, undo and split

- Stable ID: P2-09
- Executor: Luna
- Kind: Implementation
- Parent: P2
- Blocked by: P2-06

Active under the 2026-09-28 working plan; direct prerequisites below replace phase-wide release gates.

## Outcome and requirement

Store reversible human-confirmed anonymous identities and optional names.

## Design and interfaces

Reuse SQLite decisions and membership. Prevent transitive different-person contradictions transactionally; keep stale diarization mappings explicit. Undo/split preserve history and require actual human choices. Candidate similarity alone never changes membership.

Use the [working plan](../README.md), relevant [design contract](../SYSTEM-DESIGN.md) and [evaluation guidance](../EVALUATION.md). Earlier closed issue checklists are historical, not new prerequisites.
