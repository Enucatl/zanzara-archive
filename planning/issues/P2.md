## Outcome and requirement

Release **Three-model voice retrieval and identity review** only after integrated behavior, current evidence and user review satisfy this phase. This parent is a human release gate, never an unattended Luna implementation issue.

## Design and interfaces

Read [system design](../SYSTEM-DESIGN.md), [evaluation gate E7](../EVALUATION.md#e7--gates-and-expansion-checks), [review skill](../REVIEW-SKILL.md), and [execution rules](../GITHUB-SETUP.md#g7--luna-implementation-handoff-and-release-rules). The children below supply the exact bounded implementations.

P2 is a voice-retrieval and identity phase. Its frozen-20 voice artifacts are
independent of transcript availability, so its operational P1 prerequisite is
retained for model/runtime, diarization and reusable voice-service evidence;
it does not acquire a duplicate P1R transcription gate. Consumers that score
or release transcription quality depend on P1R directly. See
[P1R-01-MIGRATION](../P1R-01-MIGRATION.md).
