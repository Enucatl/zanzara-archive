## Bounded steps

1. Process remaining frozen-20 ASR/attribution as needed, then chunk at speaker change, 200 words or 60 seconds.
2. Store exact ordered word references and source offsets; build FTS5 unicode61 lexical rows and Qdrant dense points.
3. Implement idempotent indexing and generation publication with lexical availability during dense failures.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Attributed words and BGE-M3 adapter; output canonical chunks, FTS5 rows and published dense generation. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Invalid attribution provenance or embedding truncation blocks affected indexing.

## Exclusions

No reranker, automatic transcript correction or corpus expansion.
