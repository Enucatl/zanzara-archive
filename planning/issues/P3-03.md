# [P3-03] Speaker-aware transcript chunks and SQLite FTS5

- Stable ID: P3-03
- Executor: Luna
- Kind: Implementation
- Parent: P3
- Blocked by: P1-04

Active under the 2026-09-28 working plan; direct prerequisites below replace phase-wide release gates.

## Outcome and requirement

Populate existing text_chunks and text_chunks_fts from attributed transcripts; make current episodes searchable without embeddings.

## Design and interfaces

Reuse canonical word IDs, original offsets and episode-speaker boundaries; retain unassigned words and overlap flags. Bound chunks by the existing 200-word/60-second limits. Rebuilding is idempotent and updates FTS rows without stale duplicates.

Use the [working plan](../README.md), relevant [design contract](../SYSTEM-DESIGN.md) and [evaluation guidance](../EVALUATION.md). Earlier closed issue checklists are historical, not new prerequisites.
