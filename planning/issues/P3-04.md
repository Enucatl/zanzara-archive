## Bounded steps

1. Implement all three modes with episode/date/speaker filters applied before candidate limits.
2. Fuse top 100 lexical and dense lists with reciprocal rank k=60, chunk deduplication and stable ties.
3. Return excerpts and timestamped word/media references, requested/effective mode and visible lexical fallback reason.
4. Supply blinded pooled-candidate exports and `zanzara evaluation validate-text-queries --queries ... --split ...` for P3-06, checking the 40-query categories, judgments and fixed splits without generating human relevance labels.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Published chunk/FTS/dense indexes and query/filter inputs; output /api/v1/search and CLI with reproducible ranking. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Incorrect filter membership, silent degraded behavior or nondeterministic rank ties block acceptance.

## Exclusions

No unreviewed relevance claims, hidden reranking or search-time index-space mixing.
