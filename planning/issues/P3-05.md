## Bounded steps

1. Probe documented model/provider availability, Italian, actual timing and current pricing before paid dispatch.
2. Implement atomic upper-bound reservations, reconcile costs/ambiguous requests and route all retries through the US$10 ledger.
3. Test default new generations and the explicit D8 equivalence gate for any requested provider reuse; unsupported timing remains text-only.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Pinned inference client, local services and capped private credentials; output capability/equivalence reports and enforced ledger. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Unknown prices, insufficient credit, missing model capability or ambiguous costs block the relevant paid operation.

## Exclusions

No unbounded live tests, alternate voice encoders or separate free retry budget.
