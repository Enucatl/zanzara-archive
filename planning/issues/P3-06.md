## Bounded steps

1. Write eight Italian queries in each E4 category and review candidate chunks plus manually found relevant material.
2. Grade 0/1/2, record exact word references, blind pooled results to system/rank and freeze judgments before metric comparison.
3. Freeze 20 development/20 held-out queries with four per category in each; document no-result cases and judgment coverage.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior
