## Bounded steps

1. Freeze identical stratified ten-by-two-minute golden development intervals and compare Parakeet, MAI and Voxtral under the remaining budget.
2. Evaluate lexical/dense/hybrid on frozen text query splits with nDCG@10, Recall@10, MRR and no-result diagnostics.
3. Publish aggregate HTML/JSON with capabilities, exclusions, paired uncertainty, cost reconciliation and baseline deltas.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Reviewed references/query sets, provider probes and shared ledger; output paired ASR and retrieval comparison reports. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Budget/capability blockers or failed text target require remediation; local results alone do not imply the cloud comparison finished.

## Exclusions

No corpus expansion, unavailable-model substitution or cloud benchmark result fabrication.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Hybrid held-out nDCG@10 matches/exceeds lexical on identical judgments.
- [ ] ASR comparisons share audio/masks; text-only providers do not receive timing scores.
- [ ] Total paid probes/comparisons/retries remain <=US$10 and outstanding reservations are explained.
- [ ] Required evidence below is linked and applies to the reviewed commit/configuration.

Add the named focused tests/CLI commands in this issue if they do not yet exist; P0-01 establishes common uv/Ruff/pytest commands. Run relevant CPU checks plus the explicitly required real GPU/integration/evaluation actions. Do not treat a mocked model response as measured model quality.
