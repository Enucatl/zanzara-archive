## Outcome and requirement

Archive speaker API, three-model services and uploaded audio; output asynchronous voice-query workflow.

## Design and interfaces

## Prerequisites

Parent: [P4](P4.md). Required completed blockers: [P3](P3.md), [P4-01](P4-01.md). The preceding phase must have a current Sol PASS and explicit user release. This issue does not depend on its own parent closing.

## Bounded steps

1. Implement archive-speaker selection and multipart uploads for WAV/FLAC/MP3/Opus-Ogg/WebM with streaming 25 MB and decoded 60-second limits.
2. Decode locally with resource/time bounds, require one selected diarized speaker and reject <3 seconds clean speech.
3. Run full ensemble through queued jobs, display candidates/audio/model scores and delete upload-derived content one hour after receipt, including recovery/abandonment.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Archive speaker API, three-model services and uploaded audio; output asynchronous voice-query workflow. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Unbounded upload processing, retained expired data or automatic enrollment blocks acceptance.

## Exclusions

No automatic single-speaker assumption for mixed clips, retention extension or two-encoder fallback.
