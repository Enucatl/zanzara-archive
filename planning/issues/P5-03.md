## Bounded steps

1. Back up SQLite consistently and preserve artifacts/model locks; rebuild vector collections from original stored vectors.
2. Restore into fresh isolated local volumes, verify identities/history, counts/hashes, search results and generation pointers.
3. Interrupt representative jobs before/after artifact publication and resume with leases/fencing; document actual recovery times and limitations.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Populated frozen-20 state/artifacts and fresh local restore volumes; output recovery commands and measured drill evidence. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Failed restore, missing source vectors or broken recovery semantics blocks release.

## Exclusions

No destructive restore over the live archive/state or unverified backup-success claims.

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Restored canonical data and identity decisions match originals; lexical and voice searches function.
- [ ] Rebuild does not need re-inference and does not mix generations.
- [ ] Killed jobs resume without duplicate commits or lost decisions.
- [ ] Required evidence below is linked and applies to the reviewed commit/configuration.
