## Bounded steps

1. Process ASR/attribution/chunks/text-index stages in frozen priority order, preserving existing voice artifacts.
2. Validate each batch and relevant regression checks before publishing its searchable generation; pause on failures for remediation.
3. Persist resumable progress, resource/cost accounting and remaining coverage; keep old searchable generations usable until publication.

## Inputs, outputs and failure behavior

Approved queue, reusable diarization and ten-episode batches; output incremental attributed transcripts/search generations. Preserve source/model/configuration provenance in outputs and fail without publishing partial artifacts when validation fails. Failed batch/generation checks block the next batch and require remediation.

## Exclusions

No silent priority changes, unbounded batches or weakened quality targets.
